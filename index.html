AMR Hunters Overview

Welcome to the AMR hunters database! AMR hunters serves as a comprehensive repository housing antimicrobial resistance genes and their variants in bacteria. Within its curated collection, users can explore insights into amino acid changes leading to mutant variants, protein ID, gene names and their variants, antibiotic resistance profiles, gene and protein sequences in FASTA format, perform BLAST searches, access PubMed Identifiers (PMIDs), and more.
